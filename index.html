The Fighter

ACTION / STYLIZED 3D

An animated action short exploring strength, expression and the rhythm of combat. Physical performance, cinematic lighting and original music build the intensity of each movement.

Full production by Elder Baby Studio. Concept art, sculpting, retopology, texturing, rigging, animation, lighting, rendering and original music.

Ito, the Little Toad

CHARACTER / STYLIZED 3D

A character full of attitude, warmth and personality. Soft shapes, hand-painted textures and expressive animation come together to build the charm of his world.

Full production by Elder Baby Studio. From concept art and 3D development to rigging, animation, lighting, rendering, original music and sound effects.

You Will Be Back

ACTING / MUSICAL

An animated reinterpretation of an existing stage work. Character acting, facial expression and musical timing bring theatrical performance into a 3D setting.

Animation, lighting and rendering by Elder Baby Studio. Created with third-party assets, adapted to the visual direction of the project.

Versus

ACTION / ANIME-INSPIRED 3D

Fast combat, extreme poses and cameras that follow the impact. A custom toon shader combines the visual language of anime with the volume and depth of stylized 3D.

Animation, lighting, rendering, shaders and original music by Elder Baby Studio. Produced with existing assets, adapted to an original art direction.
